Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

How do revision rounds work?

+
Every project includes two complete rounds of focused revisions. We use timestamped review links so you can leave frame-accurate notes directly on the video player without messy email threads.

What is your typical turnaround timeline?

+
Short-form reels are delivered within 24–48 hours. Long-form YouTube cuts typically take 3–5 business days depending on footage scope and VFX complexity. Rush deliveries are available upon request.

How do I deliver high-resolution footage?

+
You can upload raw files via Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, or Massive.io. I handle raw camera formats (Sony S-Log, Canon C-Log, RED, ProRes, BRAW) and organize media seamlessly into my editing RAID.

What are your billing and payment terms?

+
A standard 50% deposit locks in your spot on my production calendar, with the remaining 50% due upon delivery of final clean master files. Invoices are settled securely via Stripe, Wire, or Crypto.
